# Thermotect

**Smart Building Engineering Platform | AI-Powered Cloud Solution**

Thermotect is a next-generation building engineering ecosystem that empowers architects, engineers, and construction teams with intelligent tools — all in one unified platform. ([thermotect.com][1])

---

## 🌟 What We Are

Thermotect is a smart building solutions platform that combines:

✨ **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** for design insights and technical guidance
☁️ **Cloud computing** for fast processing and rendering
📐 **Integrated engineering workflows** from design to compliance
📊 **Standards-aware calculations and regulations support**

All delivered through a single platform that helps professionals design, validate, optimize, and manage building projects more efficiently.

---

## 🔍 What You Can Do With Thermotect

* Chat with AI for **technical answers, optimization, and guidance**
* Receive **drawing edits & design improvements**
* Automate **regulation-aware building calculations**
* Execute **design, calculation, visualization, and project management** in one workspace
* Work with valid **International codes & standards** and even **National Building Regulations of Iran**, tailored by location and climate conditions.
